AL PrimeCore — Principles
1. Build With Purpose

We do not build for the sake of building.

Every product, project, and experiment should solve a problem, create value, or teach us something meaningful.

2. Excellence Over Mediocrity

Good enough is rarely enough.

We strive for quality, attention to detail, and continuous improvement in everything we create.

3. Simplicity Wins

Complexity is easy.

Clarity is difficult.

We aim to simplify systems, processes, and experiences whenever possible.

4. Learn Relentlessly

Technology evolves.

People evolve.

The world evolves.

We commit to continuous learning and adaptation.

5. Share The Journey

Progress should be documented.

Successes, lessons, failures, and discoveries all contribute to growth.

We believe in learning publicly whenever possible.

6. Execution Matters

Ideas have value.

Execution creates impact.

We judge progress by what gets built, tested, improved, and delivered.

7. Stay Curious

Curiosity drives innovation.

Questions often create more value than assumptions.

We remain students, regardless of experience.

8. Leave Things Better

Every project, system, product, and interaction should leave a positive impact.

Our goal is not only to create.

Our goal is to improve.

Our Standard

If it breaks, fix it.

If it's slow, improve it.

If it's overcomplicated, simplify it.

If it can be better, keep building.
